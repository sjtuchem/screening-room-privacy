Privacy Policy for 并肩放映

并肩放映 synchronizes playback for two people watching supported Bilibili or YouTube videos in a shared room.

Information processed by the extension

The extension reads supported video page addresses, tab titles and player state to identify eligible video tabs and let you select one. Tab titles and the list of eligible tabs are processed locally. The extension saves the most recently used room server address in browser storage. It keeps room connection credentials and tab selection in browser session storage.

Information sent to the room server

When you create or join a room, the extension connects to the room server you enter. The room service processes room codes, session tokens, the selected video's identifier, playback position, play or pause state, and playback speed. The service shares the selected video's identifier and playback state with the other participant. The supplied relay keeps active room state in memory, reserves a disconnected participant's seat for about two minutes, and temporarily uses the connecting network address to limit connection rates.

The server or its hosting provider may handle network information under its own logging and retention settings. Remote connections use encrypted WebSockets. The extension does not send your Bilibili or YouTube passwords, cookies, or video stream to the room server.

Purpose and controls

The extension uses this information to connect participants, synchronize playback and recover from brief disconnects. The extension contains no advertising or analytics. You can leave the room through the extension and clear its saved settings by removing it. Each video platform handles its own sign-in and streaming under its own privacy policy.

For privacy questions, use the publisher support contact listed on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons page for 并肩放映.
